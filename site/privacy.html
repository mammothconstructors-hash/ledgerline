Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This policy explains what LedgerLine (operated by Kevin Rodrigue) collects, why, and your choices. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to let you sign in. Login is handled by Cloudflare Access, which emails you a one-time code and sets a sign-in cookie that lasts 30 days.
  • Billing details, when paid plans open. Payments are processed by Stripe; we receive your email, plan and subscription status, never your full card number.
  • Messages you send us, such as bug reports. The Report a problem button opens your own email program with the stock, tab, app version, browser and time filled in; nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • When you open the app, at most once every 30 minutes: your sign-in email and the time, so we can see how the beta is used (who is active and how often). It is not shared and never includes which stocks you look at.
  • Questions you ask Ask AI, and plain-English screens you type. The text is sent to Anthropic, the AI provider, together with passages from the company’s SEC filings, to write the answer. LedgerLine does not save your questions; we count how many are asked per day (by network address, not by name) to keep usage within limits. Anthropic does not use API requests to train its models.
  • Basic request logs kept by our hosting provider (Cloudflare), such as IP address, time and page requested, used for security and to keep the Service running.

What stays on your device

Your saved list, notes, valuation inputs and display settings are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes them.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, or third-party analytics.
  • We do not record which stocks you look at beyond ordinary server logs.

Service providers

We share only what each needs to work: Cloudflare (hosting, login and logs), Stripe (payments, once billing opens) and Marketstack (we request prices from it; no personal information is sent). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your email and subscription records while your account is active and as long as needed for tax and legal records afterwards. Hosting logs are kept for a short period by Cloudflare.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account by emailing kgrodrigue@yahoo.com. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, which we will honor.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Access to the app requires a one-time email code, all traffic is encrypted, and we never handle card numbers. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and tell you by email for significant changes.
