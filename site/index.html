What you get

Fourteen tabs, one answer.

Eight key attributes

Growth, free cash flow, debt, price, capital efficiency, leading signals and moat: pass, watch or fail, with the numbers behind each.

Undervalued, fair or overvalued

Fair value, buy-below price and the return at today’s price, from the stock’s own ten-year normal multiple.

A verdict

Buy, Wait for price, Hold, No new money or Avoid, from a fixed rule you can read. No black box.

The full filings

Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and comprehensive income for up to 17 years, split-adjusted, plus trailing twelve months.

Notes, MD&A and risk factors

Every note to the financial statements, management’s discussion, risk factors and legal proceedings from the latest 10-K, one click each.

Forecast and the odds

A chart of price against what earnings justify, ten years back and up to six forward, plus 25,000 simulations from the filings that give the odds of each return.

Timing

Risk on or risk off from the moving averages, stretch from each average and real volume numbers: today, yesterday and the 10-, 30- and 90-day averages.

Stock explorer

S&P 500 screens run on the filings: cash machines, shrinking share counts, dividend raisers, net cash fortresses, compounders and fast growers. Plus lists by industry, income and theme, and what the superinvestors own.

Against the market

Every stock measured against SPY, QQQ, DIA or IWM over every period from one day to ten years.

40+ ratios, every year

Valuation, efficiency, debt and return ratios for every fiscal year on file, marked against their own ten-year median, with CSV download.

Questions

Before you start

Where does the data come from?

Financial statements are read from the XBRL tags in each company’s SEC EDGAR filings; notes and MD&A come from the same filings. Prices are 15-minute delayed during market hours and end-of-day otherwise, from a licensed market-data provider.

Which stocks are covered?

US-listed companies that file 10-Ks and 10-Qs with the SEC. Most foreign listings and OTC names aren’t covered.

How do I log in?

Use the email address you were invited with. We send a one-time code to that address; there is no password to remember. Each login lasts 30 days.

How does the free trial work?

During the beta there is nothing to pay. When paid plans open, new accounts get 21 days free with a card on file; you are charged on day 22 unless you cancel first. Cancel any time from Account inside the app and you keep access to the end of the period you paid for.

Is this investment advice?

No. LedgerLine is for information only. It rates stocks by fixed rules applied to public data, doesn’t know your situation and isn’t a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Trade at your own risk, and talk to a licensed financial adviser or CPA before investing.

Data. Financial data is read from XBRL tags in SEC EDGAR 10-K and 10-Q filings. Market cap and per-share figures use the latest filed share count; for companies with more than one share class, shares are converted to the class shown and may differ from other sources. Intraday prices are live from IEX, a single US exchange, through Marketstack (updated every couple of minutes), so they can differ from the consolidated market price; outside market hours prices are end-of-day closes. Scores, fair values and verdicts are produced automatically by fixed rules and can be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Verify figures against the original filings before relying on them.

Forecasts and simulations. Forecast values, IRRs and the Outlook’s odds are hypothetical results of a model run on a company’s past filings. They are not predictions or guarantees, they do not reflect actual trading, and they cannot account for everything that will affect a stock. Actual results will differ, possibly by a lot.

Screens and lists. Stock explorer screens use a snapshot of filing data dated on the screen. Superinvestor holdings come from 13F filings, which are reported up to 45 days after each quarter ends and may no longer be held. Inclusion on any screen or list is not a recommendation.
