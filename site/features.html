Data. Financial data is read from XBRL tags in SEC EDGAR 10-K and 10-Q filings. Market cap and per-share figures use the latest filed share count; for companies with more than one share class, shares are converted to the class shown and may differ from other sources. Intraday prices are delayed at least 15 minutes and come from IEX, a single US exchange, through Marketstack, so they can differ from the consolidated market price; outside market hours prices are end-of-day closes. Scores, fair values and verdicts are produced automatically by fixed rules and can be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Verify figures against the original filings before relying on them.

Forecasts and simulations. Forecast values, IRRs and the Outlook’s odds are hypothetical results of a model run on a company’s past filings. They are not predictions or guarantees, they do not reflect actual trading, and they cannot account for everything that will affect a stock. Actual results will differ, possibly by a lot.

Screens and lists. Stock explorer screens use a snapshot of filing data dated on the screen. Superinvestor holdings come from 13F filings, which are reported up to 45 days after each quarter ends and may no longer be held. Inclusion on any screen or list is not a recommendation.
